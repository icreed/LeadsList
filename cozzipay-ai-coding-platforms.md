# Cozzipay: AI coding platforms (100 tools)

Research date: 2026-11-25. Method: web search and page checks per tool (pricing pages, docs, help centres, press and event pages), with sources linked per row. 114 records from five research batches were merged into 105 unique tools and the five weakest were dropped. Full research and draft outreach copy are in `cozzipay-ai-coding-platforms.xlsx`.

**In Africa** means at least one of: an office, team or legal entity in Africa; African-currency pricing or a PPP discount for African countries; an African local payment method; an official African reseller or partnership; or an official Africa-focused programme for the product. Parent-company presence counts only when linked to the product; global card acceptance or African users alone do not count. **Not yet in African market** means no such evidence was found in our searches, not confirmed absence.

Counts: 10 'In Africa', 90 'Not yet in African market'. Evidence strength: Strong (office, local-currency pricing or local payment method for the tool itself), Moderate (official programme or partnership, or merchant-side local payments), Weak (a single community or virtual event), None found.
